The company behind the platform

Shafi Consultancy Limited

Metalytics is developed by Shafi Consultancy Limited, a specialist contract research organisation that does one thing and does it deeply: statistical programming for pharmaceutical and biotechnology companies.

Across 25 years the team has delivered CDISC-compliant SDTM and ADaM datasets, tables, listings and figures for Phase I to Phase IV studies — working both directly for sponsors and as a programming partner to other CROs.

That delivery experience is the raw material for this platform. Every feature answers a question a programmer, a reviewer or an auditor actually asked on a live study.

Why it matters that programmers built it

Most clinical reporting tools are designed by software teams and handed to statistical programmers. Metalytics was built the other way round: the people who specified it had already written the macros, chased the traceability and sat through the review cycles.

So it starts from the things that genuinely cost a programming team time:

  • Programs rewritten from scratch because nobody could find the last one
  • Outputs that cannot be traced back to the data when a regulator asks
  • No single view of where a study’s programs and outputs have actually got to
  • Review cycles that repeat because programs are hard for a reviewer to read
  • Standards applied inconsistently from one study to the next

If you have spent a submission season solving those by hand, you already know what you want out of a tool like this. See how that works in practice on the Pharma page, or across other sectors on Automation.
